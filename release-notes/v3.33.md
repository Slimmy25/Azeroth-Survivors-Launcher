# v3.33

Cloud Save can now sync with Dropbox too, and you sign in from your phone, so a Steam Deck never has to leave Game Mode. It is part of STEAM DECK EXPERIMENTAL: turn that on, press SIGN IN WITH DROPBOX in Options > Cloud Save, then open the address shown on your phone and enter the code.

- After you approve on Dropbox's page, the launcher picks up the sign-in by itself and asks you to confirm the account email.
- The launcher only uses its own folder in your Dropbox, Apps > Azeroth Survivors Launcher.
- The sync works as with Google Drive: download before PLAY, upload after, the same conflict questions and ZIP backups. Signing in while using a sync folder copies its saves into an empty Dropbox folder.
- USE DROPBOX switches back without signing in again; SIGN OUT disconnects the launcher from your Dropbox.
- Cloud Save's messages now say which service they mean.

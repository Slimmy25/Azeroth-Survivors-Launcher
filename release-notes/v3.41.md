# v3.41

The controller now shows where you are in the Wiki and Handbook side trees: a blue highlight moves with the right stick, and A opens the highlighted entry with a sliding page turn. Stats pages now turn up and down, and the Handbook tree has room between its lines.

- Controller in the Wiki and Handbook trees: the right stick moves a blue highlight up and down the side tree, starting at the page you are on. A opens the highlighted entry; until then the page stays where it is.
- Controller: opening a tree entry with A now slides the page, up when the entry is below where you are and down when it is above, the same animation as Stats.
- Controller in Stats: pages now turn with the right stick down (next) and up (previous), sliding up and down instead of sideways.
- New Player Handbook: the side tree has room between its lines, more between topics and less between a topic's pages, as in the Wiki.

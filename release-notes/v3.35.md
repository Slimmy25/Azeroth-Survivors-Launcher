# v3.35

The Stats section has a new look in the launcher's classic style: you can see the launcher's background through it, the numbers are big and gold on bronze plates, and Overview and History have new at-a-glance parts. Every tab still shows the same stats as before.

- See-through section: the launcher's night-sky picture shows behind Stats, with its cards as dark glass and bronze edges.
- Stat plates with big gold numbers (victories in green), and your win rate as a gold ring in the header.
- Overview: "Classes you play" (a bar a class, the bright part its victories) and a "Last 5 weeks" play strip.
- History: a card for each day, and a "Your last 10 runs" summary of victories, defeats, average time and best level.

# v3.32

Cloud Save can now sync with Google Drive directly, so a Steam Deck in Game Mode keeps its saves in step with your PC without any sync app. It is part of STEAM DECK EXPERIMENTAL: turn that on, then press SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE in Options > Cloud Save and enter the code shown on a phone or PC.

- The launcher only sees its own folder in your Google Drive, "Azeroth Survivors Launcher Cloud Save", which you can open in Drive yourself.
- The cloud sync works as before: it downloads before PLAY and uploads after the game, with the same conflict questions and ZIP backups.
- If Google Drive can't be reached at PLAY, the launcher asks whether to play without syncing this time.
- An upload that fails is sent first at the next sync. If Drive changed in between, you choose whose progress to keep, and the other one is kept on the device.
- Signing in while using a sync folder copies that folder's saves and history into Google Drive. CHANGE FOLDER goes back to a sync folder; USE GOOGLE DRIVE returns without signing in again.

Azeroth Survivors Launcher v1.4.

Launchers already installed update themselves. New players: download `Azeroth Survivors Launcher.exe` below and put it in your game folder.

## In short
Download More only lists addons the mod team has approved: three were taken out, and BlackwellUI and LuckyOdds were added.

## All the changes

### Addons
- DBM by Gabriellus, Death Recap by Fotze and Chest GPS by Fotze are no longer in Download More: they hadn't been
  approved by the mod team beforehand. Copies already installed stay in your AddOns folder until you remove them.
- New in Download More: BlackwellUI (BUI) by Blackwell under UI and LuckyOdds by Cadoky
  and Damage Meter by Kexo under Gameplay. They come from each author's newest release. Like the other interfaces,
  only one UI can be on at a time: turning one on asks before switching the other off.

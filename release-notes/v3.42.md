# v3.42

Stats now looks and works like the New Player Handbook, with a tree of its pages on the right, and the controller's right stick can open what is inside a class (or a Handbook topic) in the side tree.

- Controller in the Wiki and Handbook trees: right on a highlighted entry opens its own branch (a class's Spells, Talents and Gear, a Handbook topic's pages); left closes it again or moves the highlight up to the entry above it. With nothing highlighted, left and right still fold the whole tree.
- Stats is laid out like the New Player Handbook: the page on the left and a Wiki-style tree of the Stats pages on the right (Overview to Build Journal, with your classes under Classes). Click an entry or use the mouse wheel over the tree; with a controller, the left stick scrolls the page, the right stick moves the blue highlight and A opens it, sliding up or down.

# Azeroth Survivors Launcher v3.09

- AZS Minor Mods now lets you independently move, resize and scale the XP bar, chat, stored-level button, clock, encounter bars and event announcements. Supported bars can switch between horizontal and vertical layouts.
- The movable run clock keeps its next-boss countdown beside it. Encounter bars track live health for active bosses, guardians and elites, and stay visible when their group grows or the display is shorter.
- Event announcements use the game's localized banner text, remain visible for 10 seconds, and fade in and out. They have their own movable control, separate from “Move to Begin.”
- Fresh installs receive the addon fonts, font license and “Baby Crying” sound file with AZS Minor Mods.

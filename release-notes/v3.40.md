# v3.40

The controller feels more like a real pointer, and the New Player Handbook now reads like the Wiki: passage on the left, a tree of its topics on the right, with the same controller controls, and Stats pages turn with the right stick.

- Controller: the Windows pointer is hidden while the blue ball stands in for it (moving a mouse brings it back). The ball is a little bigger, and over anything you can click it grows slightly and glows beneath.
- Controller in Wiki class articles and Handbook pages: the left stick scrolls the passage, the right stick moves up and down the side tree, and pushing it left or right closes and opens the tree's branches.
- New Player Handbook: every Handbook page has the passage on the left and a Wiki-style tree of the Handbook's topics (and the open topic's pages) on the right. A click, a wheel notch or the controller moves through it.
- Controller in Stats: the left stick scrolls the page, and the right stick left and right turns to the previous or next Stats page (Overview, Classes, Abilities ... Build Journal). The new page slides in from the right going forward and from the left going back, one page at a time.

# Azeroth Survivors Launcher v3.14

## Addon catalog
- Added AZS Max Hit by ghoulcompiler and AZS MoveAnything by kodfod to Download More.
- Renamed the Kexo damage-meter listing to AZSSpellMeter and the two Move UI listings to MoveAnything, with the author shown to distinguish them.
- AZSSpellMeter and AZS Max Hit are mutually exclusive. Azeroth Cursor, Big Cursor and Cursor Reticle are mutually exclusive. The rules also apply to Toggle All and when loading saved addon profiles.

## Addon pictures
- Addon cards use SurvivorsForge screenshots when available and fall back to the addon's icon. Addons with no submitted picture stay image-free.
- Hover an addon name for a small preview. Click its name for a centered gallery with previous and next controls.
- Bundled addons combine pictures from their linked SurvivorsForge records. Verified marks remain static decisions in the launcher's catalog.

## Guide and AZS Minor Mods
- Updated the in-launcher game changelog and guide data through patch 0.137.0, including Enhancement Shaman's redesigned melee talent tree and spells.
- Added a separate worn-ring and trinket row to AZS Minor Mods. It detects the player's equipped items, remembers their first observed equipped order across reloads, and can be moved, resized, hidden, or detached from the spell/action row.

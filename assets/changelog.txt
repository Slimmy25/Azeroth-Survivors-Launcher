Patch notes shown in the launcher's Changelog. The launcher sorts them by version number itself.
Every post starts with its title on the first line. Posts are separated by a line with only === on it.

0.131.0
-------
Choose how enemy nameplates sit in a crowd, skip a level-up or a cache with a key, and see your own time of day under the minimap.

Options -> Combat has a new Nameplate Layout setting. Stacking (as before) moves nameplates apart so each one can be read; Overlapping keeps every plate right over its enemy. The change shows at once, even mid-run.
When a hand of cards can be skipped, the X key now does the same as the Skip button, and the button shows its key. Rebind it under Key Bindings -> Azeroth Survivors -> Skip. It only acts on the cards, so X still makes your hero sit or stand everywhere else.
A small clock under the minimap shows your computer's or phone's local time: 10:42 PM in English, 22:42 in the other languages. It moves and hides with the minimap and follows the HUD Scale setting.

0.130.0
-------
When you fall, the end screen now shows a Death Recap: what killed you, who dealt it and how hard it hit, with a full breakdown of your last seconds one click away.

- Under "You have fallen" you now see the killing blow: its icon and name, the enemy or boss who dealt it, the damage and any overkill.
- The new Death Recap button opens your final blows, oldest to last, each with how long before your death it landed, how much it hit for, what your shields absorbed and how much health it left you.
- Repeated hits from the same enemy are grouped (Melee x6) so a crowd does not drown out the blow that mattered.
- A Damage by Source section ranks everything that hurt you in your last seconds, with each one's share.
- Boss mechanics you could have stepped out of (telegraphed blasts, pools, orbs) are marked Avoidable, and the recap tells you how much of the damage came from them.
- Works in co-op too: each player gets their own recap of their own fall. Close it with its button or Escape.
- Amulets no longer promise one last extra target at a far-off rank (95, 106 or 109). That target came from a rounding quirk rather than real growth, and it is gone from the game as well as the tooltip. Every other target milestone is unchanged.

0.125.0
-------
Co-op lobbies get a party chat and show each player's ping, and a co-op run now needs at least two players. Co-op is easier: fewer extra enemies per player, enemies 20% weaker in a party, and revives faster with more helpers. Lower sets at +5 now gain Tier 3's stats, Tier 3 armor's echo is halved, and host and guests all need this version.

* Party chat: the co-op window now has a chat beside the party, open while you wait in the lobby (it closes when the run begins). Names show in their class colour with the class icon, staff names in their shimmering purple, and the chat notes who joins and who leaves. Type and press Enter, or click Send. Messages are up to 160 characters, and the Co-op button shows how many came in while the window was closed.
* Each guest's ping to the host shows next to their name in the lobby: green when quick, gold when fair, red when slow.
* A co-op run needs at least two players: a host alone sees "Waiting for players" until someone joins.
* The co-op window is wider in a party, with bigger class icons, and each player's race and class on their own line.
* Co-op is easier: each extra player brings 35% fewer extra enemies than before, and in a party every enemy (mobs, elites, guardians, bosses) has 20% less health and hits 20% softer. Playing alone is unchanged.
* Reviving together is faster: each extra ally reviving the same fallen friend speeds it up by 20% (two allies 1.2x, three 1.4x, four 1.6x).
* Mages now heal for 0.5% of all the damage they deal.
* Tier 3 armor's echo is halved (at +5: 6.7% to 3.4% a piece). Tier 3 weapons are unchanged.
* Lower sets at +5 come close to Tier 3: the last star now gives their armor the stats Tier 3 has and they lack (echo on every set; common pieces also get Tier 3's extra stat). A +5 set is 90% of a +5 Tier 3 for common, 92% uncommon, 94% rare, 96% epic and 98% PvP (PvP was 94%; the others were 95%).
* The armory no longer shows a set bonus when a weapon is selected: weapons don't have one.

0.124.0
-------
Danger Money and Hazard Pay unlock as they should, and a new co-op achievement, The Insane, opens the Unholy Weapon glow. Stuns, roots, fears and polymorph now wear off when used over and over, casters can no longer shoot you through walls, and Divine Shield can no longer stay up forever. Co-op parties of three to five stay in sync, and host and guests all need this version.

* Co-op parties stay in sync: when the co-op servers were at their busiest, a little of what your host sent you could get lost on the way, and each lost piece put your game further out of step with your host's. That is why it got worse the longer a run went and the bigger the party was. Nothing gets lost now: anything that goes missing is sent again, and at worst you see a short hitch.
* Fixed guests who got stuck as if "immortal": they couldn't attack, enemies ignored them, no level-up cards came and the end-of-run window never showed. The host was still waiting for them to pick cards they never received. If the cards don't arrive, your game now asks the host for them again.
* Fixed guests seeing enemies that were already gone, missing enemies that were there, and chests that wouldn't open after the connection had lost something along the way.
* Joining a party on a bad connection no longer leaves you waiting forever for the host to bring you in.
* Level-up cards no longer pop back up after you picked one when the connection stalls for a few seconds.
* Danger Money now counts everything one boss pays you, including the marks from its duplicate drops. Hazard Pay counts every mark you earn in the run, duplicates too, even ones you've already spent.
* Rest in Photosynthesis now needs Heroic III: Kel'Thuzad on any other difficulty doesn't count.
* New co-op achievement, The Insane: survive for 40 minutes or longer in Naxxramas on Heroic V with at least two other players, still standing at 40:00. It opens the Unholy Weapon glow, which shows in the armory's Weapon glow choices, locked until you earn it.
* Crowd control wears off: each enemy takes stuns, roots, fears and polymorph at full length the first time, then 80%, 60%, 40% and 20%, and is then immune to all of them for 8 seconds before it starts over. Every player's crowd control counts toward the same enemy, whatever its kind. Elites and bosses are held at most 1 second before that. Slows are unchanged. This replaces the old limit of 3 seconds in a row.

1. 

* Casters no longer shoot you through walls and buildings, such as Arathi Basin's: they walk around to a clear line first, and a bolt already in the air hits the wall if you get behind one.
* Divine Shield lasts at most 6 seconds and comes at most once every 10 seconds, whatever lowers its cooldown or casts it again (its legendary, echoes, Divine Intervention), so you are always unshielded for at least 4 seconds in between. Its tooltip shows the real numbers.
* Warrior shouts in co-op: Improved Demoralizing Shout now makes enemies take more damage from your allies too, as its tooltip now says. The strongest Demoralizing Shout and Battle Shout always win: a weaker one cast later never replaces, shortens or stacks with a stronger one, and takes over for the rest of its own time when the stronger ends. Your Battle Shout tooltip shows what you really get, even from a friend's shout. Improved Demoralizing Shout no longer wipes a paladin's Judgement of the Crusader: both count.
* Warlock: Soul Link's shield holds at most 10% of your max health (was 25%), all your Voidwalkers together. Legion Gate keeps at most 4 Voidwalkers and 6 Felhunters out at once, rolling another demon instead.
* The armory's set progress counts a set's eight armor pieces only: weapons and their stars no longer count toward it, as they never counted toward its bonuses.
* Addons: AZSZONE now has the run's Heroic affixes (affixes=sanguine,explosive, always in English), and the new AZSTOTEMS (AZS_TOTEMS, AZS_GetTotems, AZS_GetTotemCount) tells how many totems of each kind you have up, their cap and when the next one goes.

0.123.0
-------
Co-op holds together on a laggy connection: enemies no longer freeze and jump, the game no longer teleports you back during lag, and level-up cards no longer come back or pick the wrong card. Host and guests all need this version.

* Enemies keep moving smoothly when the connection to your host hiccups. They used to stand still for a moment and then jump to where they really were; now they catch up quickly instead.
* Other players jump much less after a lag spike. Their position is carried on to where they really are now, not where they were when the delay started.
* Guests are no longer teleported back on their own during lag ("stuck" indoors, or snapping back after the game corrected your position), and other players no longer see you jump for it.
* Level-up cards no longer reappear after you picked, or keep coming back over and over, on a slow connection. A pick that arrives late can no longer take a card from a newer offer you haven't seen yet.

0.121.0
-------
Heroic difficulty and gear upgrades: beat zones on Heroic I to V, earn marks from every boss, and upgrade your gear with stars. Full sets change how one of your class's spells plays, and any set can stay viable at the end.

* Heroic I to V replaces Run Options: pick it with the Difficulty button beside Play, which shows each level's enemy health and damage, rewards and twists. Each level makes the horde and bosses tougher and adds two twists: elite packs, relentless bosses, stronger guardians, a leaner purse, horde affixes, the old tower and gaze mechanics, harsher failed mechanics, halved healing, and a final boss that brings a friend.
* Heroic I of a zone opens once any of your classes beats it, and beating Heroic N there opens the next level for every class. A class can play a zone's Heroic once it has beaten that zone itself.
* Horde affixes from Heroic III, changing every week (Heroic V has two at once): Bolstering (each death makes the enemies within 12 yards 20% tougher and stronger, stacking up to triple), Sanguine (slain elites leave wide pools that heal the horde 10% a second) and Explosive (an orb every 15 seconds that bursts for 40% of your health unless destroyed; area spells pass through it: it takes five direct hits, aimed spells and weapon swings).
* Bosses aim a little ahead of where you are running: blasts, breaths, charges, beams, volleys and rains lead you, and further on each Heroic level. Change direction instead of running in a straight line.
* Boss drop chances and talent experience rise with the Heroic level. Against the Odds and the co-op feats that asked for maxed Run Options now ask for Heroic III or harder.
* Marks: every boss pays your class marks, more in harder zones and on higher Heroic levels (normal pays half), and bosses that return in endless pay more each time. A piece you already own pays marks instead of nothing.
* Bad luck protection: each boss kill that misses a piece makes that piece likelier to drop next time.
* Star upgrades: spend marks in the armory to raise a piece from 0 to 5 stars. A set's stars open as your class beats Heroic levels in the set's zones (Heroic II opens 2 stars, and so on). Lower sets are cheaper, and every other class that has a set's armor at a star rank makes that rank 5% cheaper for yours, up to 40%.
* Each star adds to the piece's own bonuses: 5 stars make tier 3 gear 60% stronger. Lower sets gain more from each star, and their 5th star is a big one that lifts them to 95% of 5-star tier 3, so your favourite set stays viable at the end. That last star costs what tier 3's does (30 marks).
* 4-piece bonuses: four armor pieces of one set at 1 star or more give its bonus, from more gold and pickup range on the common set to more critical damage on the legendary one.
* 8-piece bonuses: all eight armor pieces of one set at 3 stars or more change how one of your class's spells plays, a different one for each set. Fireball splits, Aimed Shot pierces, Execute strikes more of the most wounded enemies and its kills cut your cooldowns, Consecration, Blizzard and Volley move with you, and so on: check each set in the armory. At 5 stars on all eight, the bonus is empowered: half again as strong.
* The PvP sets are built around crowd control: your stun, root, slow or fear spell (Hammer of Justice, Concussive Shot, Kidney Shot, Psychic Scream, Frost Nova, Death Coil, Shred) hits more enemies, harder and more often, and all your damage is higher against enemies you stun, root, slow or fear.
* Armor tooltips show their set, as in the original game: its name with how many pieces you wear, the pieces, and its 4-piece and 8-piece bonuses with the stars they need (character pane, C).
* Weapon glows: weapons at 5 stars glow with Icy Chill, and two achievements open Crusader (Champion of Naxxramas) and Fiery Weapon (Radiant Armory). Pick your class's glow, or none, on any weapon in the armory; a picked glow also replaces a legendary weapon's own.
* Armory collection: pieces still to find in your zones show as tiles with their boss and drop chance. Once you have them all, star progress takes their place: each set's stars per piece, its 4 and 8-piece bonuses and how many stars are open. Small gear bonuses show their tenths (+0.3% critical strike instead of +0%).
* New achievements for stars, set bonuses, full and empowered sets, marks, endless bosses and Heroic, with a new Heroic tab.
* In co-op, the host picks the Heroic level and every player needs it open. Everyone earns their own marks, drops and clears.
* Co-op is easier: each extra player adds 20% less enemy health, horde size and damage than before.

0.28.1
------
New pickup: the Magnet.

* Walk over a magnet and every experience shard and every coin on the whole field flies to you.
* A run has 2 or 3 magnets, spread across its 20 minutes. Bosses drop them, and so do guardians sometimes. If none has dropped for a while, one appears on the field by itself.
* A magnet is a floating blue rune standing in a pillar of blue light, so you can spot it from across the field.
* Magnets don't disappear. Leave one where it is and come back for it when the field is covered in gold.

0.27.0
------
Loot is no longer endless: rings, trinkets, amulets, soulstones and guardians all have limits.

* Rings and trinkets grow toward a ceiling. Each upgrade adds less than the one before, and a piece never goes past five common picks' worth: a ring of damage tops out at +85%, a ring of damage reduction at +25%, Hand of Justice at 30%. No more 145% damage reduction.
* A piece at its ceiling is no longer offered. If that leaves a cache or a guardian's pick short, its last card is a pouch of gold.
* Major Soulstone holds 2 lives at most. Picking it again refills it, and it only shows up once you have used a life.
* Amulets grow toward three times their numbers. The first empowering is as strong as before; each one after it adds less.
* Guardians: every guardian you slay gives the later ones 25% more health and 5% more damage, and a lair fills again after 3:00 (was 2:30). They are a prize to win a few times a run, not a farm.

0.20.0
------
* Critical strike chance can now go past 100%. Every point above 100% is added to your critical damage instead, one for one: 120% crit with 200% critical damage becomes 100% crit with 220% critical damage.
* Echo is no longer capped at 50%. Past 100%, every spell casts twice and the rest is your chance of a third cast: at 150% echo a spell always casts twice, and half the time three times.
* Spell tooltips and the character pane show the new crit and echo rules.

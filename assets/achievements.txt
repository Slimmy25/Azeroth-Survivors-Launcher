Achievements of Azeroth Survivors, read from the in-game Achievements tab (Armory) in the videos achievements.mp4 and part_3_1.mp4.
Account-wide. 131 achievements in seven groups. Points are the number shown on each achievement.
Where the game gives two achievements the same name, the label in parentheses says which one it is. The parentheses are not part of the in-game name.

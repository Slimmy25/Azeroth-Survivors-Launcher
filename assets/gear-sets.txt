@four   rarity | text | stat=value, ...    the 4-piece bonus, the same for every class (4 armor pieces of the set at 1+
        stars). The stats are those the Gear Planner can add; a bonus with a condition is text only.
@eight  class | rarity | text               the 8-piece bonus (all 8 armor pieces at 3+ stars; empowered at 5 stars on all
        eight: half again as strong). Each class's own.
Lines starting with # and everything above the first === line are ignored.

= Het pad bovenaan
This window has no rows of buttons, to give its pages as much room as possible. The path at the top does their job: each step is where you are (Guide › Classes › Priest).\n\nClick a step to go back to it. Point at a step with ▾, or click it, for the list of places beside it (the other tabs, classes or pages). The arrow on the left goes back the way you came, and the X takes you home.
= Dit venster heeft geen rijen knoppen, zodat de pagina's zoveel mogelijk ruimte krijgen. Profile 1 keeps a full copy of itself in its own isolated space, apart from Profile 2 and No profile: which addons are on, where the interface sits and every addon's settings. A heavy UI addon can change things here without touching the others, and when you come back to Profile 1 it is exactly the way you left it.
= Profiel 1 bewaart een volledige kopie van zichzelf in een eigen afgeschermde ruimte, los van profiel 2 en Geen profiel: welke addons aan staan, waar de interface zit en de instellingen van elke addon.

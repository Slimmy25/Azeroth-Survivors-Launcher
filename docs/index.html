Azeroth Survivors Launcher

A free, community-made launcher for Azeroth Survivors, the game by Aethus. It installs and starts the game, keeps your addons up to date, and can sync your save between your PCs and a Steam Deck.

What it does

  • Installs the game and starts it with PLAY.
  • Downloads and updates addons approved by the community's mod team.
  • Cloud Save: downloads your save before you play and uploads it when the game closes, through a sync folder or straight through Google Drive or Dropbox.
  • A New Player Handbook, a wiki of classes, items and talents, and planners for gear and talents.
  • Steam Deck support (experimental): one Steam entry for launcher and game, full screen in Game Mode.

Download

The newest version is on the Releases page. The launcher keeps itself up to date after that.

Google Drive

If you choose Google Drive for Cloud Save, the launcher only uses one folder it creates in your Drive, Azeroth Survivors Launcher Cloud Save, for your save and your addons' settings. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it uses and how to remove its access.

Dropbox

If you choose Dropbox for Cloud Save, the launcher only uses its own folder, Apps › Azeroth Survivors Launcher. You sign in on your phone at the Dropbox sign-in page with the code your launcher shows, so it also works in Steam's Game Mode. See the privacy policy.

Privacy policy

Azeroth Survivors Launcher · last updated 6 November 2026

Azeroth Survivors Launcher is a free, community-made program that runs on your own computer or Steam Deck. It has no accounts and no servers of its own: the developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

Google Drive (only if you sign in)

Cloud Save can sync with Google Drive. This is optional and off until you choose SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE. If you do, the launcher asks Google for two permissions:

  • Its own files in your Google Drive (drive.file). The launcher creates one folder, Azeroth Survivors Launcher Cloud Save, and only reads and writes the files it put there: your game save, your addons' settings, and backups of earlier versions of them. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address (email), only to show which Google account is signed in.

Dropbox (only if you sign in)

Cloud Save can also sync with Dropbox. This is optional and off until you choose SIGN IN WITH DROPBOX. The launcher uses Dropbox's App folder access: Dropbox gives it one folder, Apps › Azeroth Survivors Launcher, and it cannot see or change anything else in your Dropbox. It also reads your account's email address, only so you can confirm that the right account was connected.

You sign in on your phone, on the launcher's sign-in page. To pass the sign-in from your phone to your launcher, the page and the launcher use ntfy.sh, a free public message relay, under a topic named after the one-time code the launcher shows. Only two short messages go through it: a one-time key check from the launcher, and the one-time sign-in code from Dropbox back to it. That sign-in code cannot be used without a secret key that never leaves your launcher, and it expires within minutes.

Where your data goes

  • Your save files travel only between your device and your own Google Drive or Dropbox.
  • The launcher's sign-in to Google or Dropbox is stored only on your device, in the launcher's settings, protected with your operating system's data protection for your user account. Your email address is stored there too, for display.
  • Nothing from your Google or Dropbox account is sent to the developer or to anyone else, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Azeroth Survivors Launcher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • In the launcher: Options › Cloud Save › SIGN OUT. This removes the sign-in from your device and asks Google to end the launcher's access.
  • In your Google account: Third-party connections, then remove Azeroth Survivors Launcher.
  • In Dropbox: Settings › Connected apps, then disconnect Azeroth Survivors Launcher. SIGN OUT in the launcher's Dropbox section does this too.
  • Your saves stay in your Drive or Dropbox folder until you delete them; you can delete that folder at any time.

Other connections the launcher makes

The launcher also downloads its own updates, addons, news, pictures and videos from GitHub, the addon creators' release pages and the sites whose pictures and videos it shows (such as YouTube and Wowhead). These requests carry no personal information beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
